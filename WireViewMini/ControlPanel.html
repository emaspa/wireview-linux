Y axis

Read from device reads the log area of the device's flash (8 MB) over the serial port, which takes about a minute and a half; with wireviewd WireView Mini borrows the port for it. The log has no clock: as in the app, the time is counted from each power-on. The result stays in WireView Mini until the next read.

Custom background image

Preview

Apply writes the colors, inversion and slots to the device like the configuration's Apply (Store to device memory, under Device configuration, keeps them after a power cycle). A custom background image is scaled here, in the browser, and written to the device's flash with matching highlight-tinted fan frames into the selected slot; the transfer takes a minute or two. Theme files (.wv2t) are the WireView app's and the official Windows app's.
